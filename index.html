I am a Manager in the Monetary Policy Department of the Reserve Bank of India. My research focuses on the transmission of monetary policy through financial markets and the broader economy.

My work combines empirical macroeconomics and quantitative methods to study how financial markets respond to policy and macroeconomic developments. This site brings together my research, professional experience.
