Monetary transmission, the yield curve, and inflation in India.

AP

I am a Manager in the Monetary Policy Department of the Reserve Bank of India. My research follows how policy decisions travel through financial markets — into bond yields, inflation expectations, and the wider economy.

I read Economics at the Madras School of Economics, specialising in applied quantitative finance, after an engineering degree at Delhi Technological University, and I am a certified Financial Risk Manager (GARP, 2024). This site collects my research, my work at the Bank, and my full CV.
